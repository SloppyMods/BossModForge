# Inference

## Overview
Turns a parsed `Recording` into structured boss-fight knowledge: it segments pulls, classifies participants, catalogs abilities/statuses/tethers/icons, fits the arena bounds, infers AoE shapes (with lag search and game-data priors), and classifies mechanics. Timeline alignment and AoE fitting primitives live in the two child directories. Namespace: `BossModForge.Core.Inference`. Typical order implied by signatures: `PullSegmenter.Segment` → `ParticipantClassifier.Classify` → `AbilityCataloger.Catalog` → `BoundsFitter.Fit` → `ShapeInference.Infer/Classify` → `MechanicClassifier.Classify` [PullSegmenter.cs:PullSegmenter.Segment, ParticipantClassifier.cs:ParticipantClassifier.Classify, AbilityCataloger.cs:AbilityCataloger.Catalog, BoundsFitter.cs:BoundsFitter.Fit, ShapeInference.cs:ShapeInference.Infer, MechanicClassifier.cs:MechanicClassifier.Classify].

## Interface
- Result primitives: `Confidence(Score, Band, Reasons)` with `Of(score, reasons...)` (clamps to [0,1]; High ≥0.85, Medium ≥0.6, else Low) and `LowBecause(...)` (score 0.3); `ConfidenceBand`; `Evidence(Kind, Summary, Data?)`; `ReviewItem`; `ReviewSeverity` [Confidence.cs:Confidence, Confidence.cs:Evidence, Confidence.cs:ReviewItem]
- Pulls: `PullOutcome` (`Kill, Wipe, Abandoned, RecordingEnded`), `Pull`, `SegmenterOptions` (`Default`: 10s min pull, 8s combat gap), `PullSegmenter.Segment(Recording, options?)` [PullSegmenter.cs:Pull, PullSegmenter.cs:SegmenterOptions, PullSegmenter.cs:PullSegmenter.Segment]
- `InferenceActors` (internal): `Index, At, Last, InCombatAt, IsDeadOrGone, IsNonPlayerNonPet, IsPlayer, OwnerIsPlayer` [PullSegmenter.cs:InferenceActors]
- Participants: `ParticipantRole`, `Participant`, `ParticipantClassifier.HelperOid = 0x233C`, `Classify(Recording, pulls, IGameData)` [ParticipantClassifier.cs:ParticipantClassifier]
- Catalog: `AbilityHit`, `AbilityInstance`, `Ability`, `StatusEntry`, `TetherEntry`, `IconEntry`, `AbilityCatalog`, `CatalogOptions` (`Default`: before 0.2s, after 2.5s, no player actions), `AbilityCataloger.Catalog(Recording, pulls, participants, IGameData, options?)` [AbilityCataloger.cs:AbilityCataloger.Catalog]
- Arena: `BoundsFitterOptions`, `ArenaKind`, `ArenaFit`, `BoundsFitter.Fit(Recording, pulls, primaryOid, options?)` and `BoundsFitter.Fit(ImmutableArray<Vector2>, Vector2? bossSpawn, options?)` [BoundsFitter.cs:BoundsFitter.Fit]
- Shapes: `ShapeKind`, `ShapeOrigin`, `ShapeDirection`, `Shape` (factories `Circle/Donut/Cone/Rect/Cross`, statics `Raidwide`, `SingleTarget`, `Contains`), `ShapePrior`, `ShapeObservation`, `ShapeFit` [Shape.cs:Shape, Shape.cs:ShapePrior, Shape.cs:ShapeObservation, Shape.cs:ShapeFit]
- `ShapeInferenceOptions`; `ShapeInference.PriorFor(ActionRow?)`, `.Fit(observations, prior, arenaRadius, options?)`, `.Infer(Ability, Recording, pulls, ArenaFit, options?)`, `.Classify(Ability, ShapeFit?, ArenaFit, options?) -> MechanicClass` [ShapeInference.cs:ShapeInference]
- `ShapeObservations.Observe(Ability, Recording, pulls, lagSeconds)`, `.HasPlayerDamage`, `.IsDamaging` [ShapeObservations.cs:ShapeObservations]
- `OmenParser.TryParseFan(omenPath, out halfAngleDeg)`; `TryParseDonut` always returns false [OmenParser.cs:OmenParser]
- `Geometry2D` (internal): `Xz, Facing, Right, Atan2, SignedAngleDeg, ConvexHull, PolygonArea, MinEnclosingCircle, DouglasPeucker` [Geometry2D.cs:Geometry2D]
- Mechanics: `MechanicClass`, `MechanicClassification`, `MechanicClassifier.RuleKind/ParamsKind/BaitKind` consts, `Classify(Ability, Recording, pulls, ArenaFit, ShapeFit?, MechanicClass shapeClass)` [MechanicClassifier.cs:MechanicClassifier]
- Subdirectories:
  - [Fitters](./Fitters/EGM.md) — per-shape hit/miss fitters and snapping used by `ShapeInference`
  - [Timeline](./Timeline/EGM.md) — anchor extraction, multi-pull alignment, loop folding, phase detection

## Invariants
- `Confidence.Of` clamps score to [0,1] [Confidence.cs:Confidence.Of]
- Pull segmentation: pulls shorter than `MinPullDuration` are dropped (recorded as `DroppedPull` evidence on the next kept pull); when `BossOid` is set, pulls with other `PrimaryOid` are removed and indices renumbered; empty ops → empty result [PullSegmenter.cs:PullSegmenter.Segment]
- Pull end priority at a timestamp: zone change (`Abandoned`) → all players dead (`Wipe`) → all enemy candidates dead/out of combat with no re-combat within `CombatGapToSplit` (`Kill` if primary dead, else `Wipe`); otherwise `RecordingEnded` at `recording.End` [PullSegmenter.cs:PullSegmenter.ScanEnd, PullSegmenter.cs:PullSegmenter.AllCandidatesResolved]
- Primary pick: most `ActorCastEventOp`s in the pull, ties by larger hitbox radius, iterating ids in ascending order; no candidates → `(0, 0)` [PullSegmenter.cs:PullSegmenter.PickPrimary]
- Participants are grouped by OID in ascending order; role precedence: Player, Pet/Chocobo/player-owned, Helper (type or OID 0x233C), EventObject, Boss (matches some `pull.PrimaryOid`), Caster (untargetable casting enemy), Add (targetable enemy), else Unknown [ParticipantClassifier.cs:ParticipantClassifier.ClassifyRole]
- Catalog output is sorted: abilities by (Oid, ActionRaw), instances by (EffectTime, SourceInstanceId), statuses/tethers/icons by id; ops outside any pull are ignored for casts/status/tether/icon; player actions excluded unless `IncludePlayerActions`; cast-start matched to the first unmatched pending cast within `[CastEnd - MatchWindowBefore, CastEnd + MatchWindowAfter]`; unmatched `CST+` add `UnmatchedCast` evidence and lower ability confidence to 0.7 [AbilityCataloger.cs:AbilityCataloger.Catalog, AbilityCataloger.cs:AbilityCataloger.ObserveCastEvent, AbilityCataloger.cs:AbilityCataloger.BuildAbilities]
- Pending casts for an actor are cleared on `ActorDestroyOp` [AbilityCataloger.cs:AbilityCataloger.ClearPending]
- `BoundsFitter.Fit(samples, ...)`: fewer than 20 distinct points → Circle radius 20 with low confidence; Circle radii snap to `[10,15,19,20,25,30]` within `RadiusSnapTolerance`, else round to 0.5; Square if `|halfW-halfH| <= 1`; sampling excludes knockback (2s after) and death (2s before) windows [BoundsFitter.cs:BoundsFitter.Fit, BoundsFitter.cs:BoundsFitter.SnapRadius]
- `Shape.Contains` for `SingleTarget`/default kinds returns false; `RaidwideNoShape` returns true [Shape.cs:Shape.Contains]
- `ShapeInference.Fit`: all-hit fraction `>= RaidwideFraction` returns raidwide; `SingleTarget` prior short-circuits to score 0.3; a prior mismatch caps score at 0.59; margin below the cap caps score at 0.84; no consistent candidate falls back to prior (score 0.3) or null [ShapeInference.cs:ShapeInference.Fit, ShapeInference.cs:ShapeInference.ScoreProposal, ShapeInference.cs:ShapeInference.PriorFallback]
- `ShapeInference.Infer` searches lag grid `[LagMinSeconds, LagMaxSeconds]` in `LagStepSeconds` steps, preferring higher consistency, then margin, then smaller `|delta|`; only instances with player damage in a valid pull index are used [ShapeInference.cs:ShapeInference.Infer, ShapeInference.cs:ShapeInference.LagGrid]
- `ShapeInference.PriorFor` maps `ActionRow.CastType` 1,2,3,4,5,7,8,10,11,12,13 to priors; other values → null [ShapeInference.cs:ShapeInference.PriorFor]
- `MechanicClassifier.Classify` rule order: RW-1, TB-1/TB-2, SP-1, ST-1, KB-1, GZ-1, TW-1, CH-1, TA-1, HA-1, IN-1, IR-1, UC-1; the first matching rule wins and its rule id is inserted as first `Evidence` [MechanicClassifier.cs:MechanicClassifier.Classify, MechanicClassifier.cs:MechanicClassifier.Done]
- `BoundsFitter.Fit(Recording, ...)` runs `DetectArenaChange`: adds `ArenaChange` evidence when the min-enclosing-circle radius shifts by more than 3 between consecutive 30s windows (needs ≥40 timed samples, ≥8 per window) [BoundsFitter.cs:BoundsFitter.DetectArenaChange]
- `BoundsFitter.Fit(samples, ...)` also produces `Rect` (hull fits a box, not a square) and `Custom` (Douglas-Peucker polygon, low confidence "custom polygon"); adds `CenterOffset` evidence (distance from centre to boss spawn) when a spawn is given; lowers the confidence band (≥0.85 → 0.84, ≥0.6 → 0.59) for Circle/Square when centre is more than 3 from the spawn [BoundsFitter.cs:BoundsFitter.Fit, BoundsFitter.LowerBand]
- `ShapeInference.Classify` rules in order: null fit or `SingleTarget` → `Unclassified`; `RaidwideNoShape` → `Raidwide`; Source-origin Circle with radius ≥ `RaidwideArenaRatio` (0.95) × `arena.Radius` → `Raidwide`; shape kind not Circle/Donut/Cone/Rect/Cross → `Unclassified`; no cast bar → `Unclassified`; no `Sheet` → `TelegraphedAOE`; `OmenId == 0` with empty `OmenPath` → `HiddenAOE`; else `TelegraphedAOE` [ShapeInference.cs:ShapeInference.Classify]
- Pull confidence: 0.9 when the end is zone change, all players dead or primary dead; 0.7 for a combat-gap end; low for recording-ended. A pull gets `NoZone` evidence when no ZONE op precedes its start [PullSegmenter.cs:PullSegmenter.ConfidenceFor, PullSegmenter.Segment]
- `ShapeInference.Fit` caps the raidwide score at 0.84 with fewer than 2 observations; a raidwide candidate competes with shape candidates only when the all-hit fraction is ≥ 0.5 [ShapeInference.cs:ShapeInference.Fit]
- Participant confidence is 0.9 for most roles and 0.75 for `Caster` [ParticipantClassifier.cs:ParticipantClassifier.ClassifyRole]
- Geometry convention: 2D uses X,Z of world positions; facing = `(sin θ, cos θ)` [Geometry2D.cs:Geometry2D.Xz, Geometry2D.cs:Geometry2D.Facing]
- `ShapeInference` carries private duplicates of `ShapeObservations`' `HasPlayerDamage`/`Observe` logic [ShapeInference.cs:ShapeInference.HasPlayerDamage, ShapeInference.cs:ShapeInference.Observe, ShapeObservations.cs:ShapeObservations]

## Side Effects
- No file, network, or database I/O; no static mutable state (only `static readonly` option defaults and lookup arrays) [Confidence.cs, PullSegmenter.cs, BoundsFitter.cs:BoundsFitter.RadiusSnaps, ShapeInference.cs:ShapeInference.KindOrder]
- Reads `IGameData` lookups (`BNpcName`, `StatusName`, `Action`) in `ParticipantClassifier.Classify` and `AbilityCataloger.Catalog` [ParticipantClassifier.cs:ParticipantClassifier.Classify, AbilityCataloger.cs:AbilityCataloger.GetAbility, AbilityCataloger.cs:AbilityCataloger.ObserveStatus]
- `AbilityCataloger.Catalog` ignores its `participants` argument (`_ = participants`); `ShapeInference.Fit` ignores `arenaRadius`; `MechanicClassifier.Classify` ignores `arena` [AbilityCataloger.cs:AbilityCataloger.Catalog, ShapeInference.cs:ShapeInference.Fit, MechanicClassifier.cs:MechanicClassifier.Classify]
- Several scans iterate all `recording.Ops` per call (e.g. `BossTargetAt`, `HasIconNear`), so cost grows with op count [MechanicClassifier.cs:MechanicClassifier.BossTargetAt, MechanicClassifier.cs:MechanicClassifier.HasIconNear]
